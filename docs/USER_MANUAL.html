Official TourneyPro Guide

🏆 TourneyPro User Manual

Complete Web Interface Guide for Tournament Directors, Umpires, Captains & Players
No technical setup required • Works seamlessly on Desktop, Tablet & Mobile • Updated November 2026

1. User Roles & Capabilities Overview

TourneyPro provides an intuitive web experience designed specifically for each role in a tennis tournament:

User Role Primary Web Capabilities
🏆 Tournament Director (Organizer) Creates tournaments, manages divisions, appoints staff, generates umpire court PINs, toggles public draw publication, and prints the Desk Check-in Roster.
👔 Tournament Staff (Manager / Official) Manages court queues, handles rain delays, updates scheduled times, adjudicates walkovers, and verifies scores.
⏱️ Court Scorer / Chair Umpire Scores tennis matches live on court using a smartphone or tablet via standard account or instant 6-digit Court PIN.
🧢 Team League Captain Submits player nominations for their team's rubbers before the 15-minute pre-match deadline.
🎾 Player / Athlete / Parent Enters tournaments online, tracks upcoming matches on Order of Play, and views personal ranking statistics.
🌐 Public Spectator / Fan Browses public tournaments, tracks live ball-by-ball scoreboards, and downloads official bracket PDFs.
💡 Pure Web Experience
Everything runs directly in your web browser. No apps to install. Works seamlessly on Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, tablets, and smartphones.

2. For Tournament Organizers & Directors

2.1 Appointing Tournament Staff & Umpires

As the tournament owner, you can authorize other registered users to score matches or manage courts without sharing your account password.

1
Log in and navigate to your tournament dashboard (/t/<slug>/manage).
2
Click on the 🛡️ Staff & Umpires tab in the navigation bar.
3
Under 👤 Appoint Staff Member, enter the user's username or email (e.g., testuser).
4
Select their role: Manager (schedules & scores), Official / Referee (rules & scoring), or Scorer (live score entry only).
5
Click ➕ Appoint Staff Member. Privileges are activated immediately!
Figure 1: Staff & Umpires Tab — Appointing Staff Members and Generating 6-Digit Court PINs

2.2 Creating a 6-Digit PIN for Court Umpires

When volunteer umpires or attendants do not have an account, generate an instant 6-digit court PIN:

1
Under ⚡ Generate Court Access PIN, type the Assigned Court (e.g., Court 2).
2
Select the role (Scorer) and duration (e.g., 24 Hours).
3
Click ⚡ Generate 6-Digit PIN. Provide the 6-digit PIN and login link (/t/<slug>/score-login) to your umpire.

2.3 Publishing the Draw (Draft vs. Public)

When generating brackets, TourneyPro keeps draws in Draft mode to prevent premature public access while seeds and byes are finalized.

Figure 2: Category Bracket Header — "📢 Publish Draw" toggle and "🔒 Draft Draw" badge
🔒 Draft Draw Protection
While in Draft Draw, public visitors who click "Export Draw PDF" receive a friendly restriction notice. Organizers can preview and test the PDF draft at any time.
1
Navigate to the category bracket: /t/<slug>/categories/<id>.
2
Review seeds, participant brackets, and byes.
3
Click the green 📢 Publish Draw button at the top header.
4
The badge turns to ✅ Published. The public can now immediately download the official Draw PDF!

2.4 Printing the Check-in Desk Roster PDF

For physical tournament registration desks, the director can export a confidential master roster with contact info and payment records.

Figure 3: Tournament Navigation Bar — "🖨️ Desk Roster PDF" visible exclusively to Directors
🖨️ One-Click Desk Copy
On the Tournament Hub (/t/<slug>?view=bracket), click 🖨️ Desk Roster PDF. A high-resolution, print-ready roster will open, ready to print for your physical registration desk.

2.5 Downloading Matches and Standings Sheets

On each category page, organizers have quick access to:

  • 📄 Matches CSV: Complete list of matches, courts, and scores for spreadsheets.
  • 📊 Standings CSV: Team points, set differentials, and qualification rankings.

3. For Court Scorers & Chair Umpires

3.1 Logging in with a 6-Digit Court PIN

Umpires do not need a username or password. Simply use the 6-digit court PIN provided by the tournament director.

Figure 4: Tablet-Optimized Court Scoring Mode Login at /t/<slug>/score-login
1
Open your mobile or tablet browser and go to /t/<slug>/score-login.
2
Enter the 6-digit Court PIN (e.g., 849201).
3
Tap ⚡ Unlock Court Scoring. You are immediately in live court scoring mode!

3.2 Entering Tennis Scores

Tap on your match fixture and click Report Score. Enter scores using standard tennis syntax:

  • Standard 2-Set Match: 6-4, 6-3
  • Tiebreak Set: 7-6(4), 6-4 (loser's tiebreak points inside parentheses)
  • Match Tiebreak (Decider): 6-4, 4-6, [10-7]
  • Select the Match Winner and click Submit Result.
⚖️ Anti-Conflict Rule
If you are playing in a match, the system automatically blocks you from recording or altering your own score. An independent official or director must record your match.

4. For Team League Captains

In team leagues, team captains nominate which players contest each individual rubber (Singles, Doubles 1, Doubles 2).

1
Log in with your player account and navigate to your team's division page.
2
Find your upcoming fixture and tap Allot Lineup.
3
Select your squad players for each rubber. (Opponent fields are protected).
4
Click Save Lineups.
⏱️ 15-Minute Deadline Rule
Lineups automatically lock 15 minutes prior to the scheduled fixture start time. Make sure to submit nominations early!

5. For Players, Parents & Spectators

Figure 5: Public Tournaments Directory — Browse events and follow live brackets
  • Online Registration: Tap ✍️ Register Now on any open tournament to enroll online.
  • Find Match Times: Tap 📋 Order of Play at the top of the tournament page to view your court number and estimated start time.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why does the Draw PDF say it's unpublished?
A: The organizer is finalizing the draw. Once published, the download button will activate automatically.

Q: Can an umpire use their smartphone on court?
A: Yes! The /score-login interface is designed specifically for mobile touchscreens and tablets.

Q: What if two umpires submit a score at the same time?
A: TourneyPro detects the conflict and asks you to tap Reload to verify the latest score.
